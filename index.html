Yayoi Kusama

A Japanese artist that makes avant-garde art, also known as the princess of polka dots. She done performance art, installations, sculpture, and paintings. She was vital part of New York’s avant-garde art scene from the late 1950s to the early 1970s. She was last based in Matsumoto, Tokyo, and New York.
