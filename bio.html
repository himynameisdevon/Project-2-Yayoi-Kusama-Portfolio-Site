Biography

Experienced hallucinations as a young child of dots and nets and other repeating patterns.

Suffered abuse and neglect as a child from her mother.

War War II (sewing Parachutes)-she would hide during air raids under tables and sketch.

1952: Staged her first solo art exhibitions in Japan (including in her home prefecture) despite family opposition to her career.

1957: Exhibited her early abstract "Infinity Nets" and works on paper in Seattle after leaving Japan.

1958: Moved to New York City, where she quickly established herself among the downtown avant-garde, Pop art, and Minimalism movements.

1965: Debuted her first landmark immersive environment, Infinity Mirror Room—Phalli's Field, at the Castellane Gallery in New York, cementing her signature style.

She lived voluntarily in a psychiatric facility for nearly half a century, utilizing it not as a prison, but as a sanctuary.
