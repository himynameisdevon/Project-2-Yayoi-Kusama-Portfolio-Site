Awards

Order of Culture (2016): Bestowed directly by the Emperor of Japan at the Imperial Palace. Kusama made history as the first woman to be honored with this medal specifically for drawings and sculptures.

Praemium Imperiale (2006): Awarded by the Japan Art Association for painting. She was the first Japanese woman to win this prize, which is widely considered the Nobel Prize of the art world.

Ordre des Arts et des Lettres (2003): Appointed as a Knight (Chevalier) by the French government for her monumental impact on global contemporary art.

International Experimental Film Prizes (1968): Her psychedelic short film, Kusama's Self-Obliteration, won prizes at major experimental circuits, including the Knokke International Film Competition in Belgium and the Ann Arbor Film Festival.
